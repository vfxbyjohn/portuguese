Expressions in Context
Tem alguma coisa errada.There's something wrong.
Mas é a minha cara.It's totally my thing / It's so me.
Já estou indo!I'm on my way!
Pois não, pode falar.Sure, go ahead.
Preciso trocar dinheiro na casa de câmbio.I need to exchange money at the exchange.
Só isso, obrigado.That's all, thanks.
Cheguei atrasado por causa do trânsito.I arrived late because of traffic.
🇧🇷 Many of these phrases pull double duty — escada rolante works at the airport and in a shopping centre, casa de câmbio works anywhere you travel. The more contexts you practise them in, the faster they stick.
Dicas de Prática
Ways to practise with a speaker
1
Airport Walkthrough
Narrate the entire airport journey out loud: check in, despachar a mala, pass through alfândega, find the portão, board. Use as many words from this page as possible.
2
Lost Traveller
One person plays a confused traveller asking for directions in the airport. The other gives directions using Siga-me, vire à direita, siga em frente, etc.
3
Monday at Work
Describe your work week using the work vocabulary: Do you work remoto? What are your prazos? How long is your commute? Have a conversation about it.
4
Expression Quick-Fire
Your partner describes a situation in English — you respond with the right expression. "You're at the register and done shopping" → "Só isso, obrigado." Speed builds fluency.
5
Disaster Story
Tell the story of a terrible travel day: you were late, missed the flight, got lost, needed directions. Use as many phrases from every section as you can.

1. Match the Portuguese to English:
a) o voo → ___
b) o portão → ___
c) despachar → ___
d) a alfândega → ___
e) aterrissar → ___
Choices: customs, to land, flight, gate, check luggage
a) o voo → flight
b) o portão → gate
c) despachar → check luggage
d) a alfândega → customs
e) aterrissar → to land
2. Translate to Portuguese:
a) "Boarding is at gate 5."
b) "I need to check my suitcase."
c) "Where's my boarding pass?"
a) O embarque é no portão 5.
b) Preciso despachar minha mala.
c) Cadê o meu comprovante de embarque?
3. Fill in the blanks:
a) Perdi o ___ porque me atrasei. (flight)
b) O avião vai ___ em 20 minutos. (land)
c) A ___ de mão vai comigo no avião. (carry-on)
a) Perdi o voo porque me atrasei.
b) O avião vai aterrissar em 20 minutos.
c) A bagagem de mão vai comigo no avião.
