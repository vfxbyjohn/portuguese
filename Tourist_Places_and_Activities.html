Describing places
O lugar é lindo.The place is beautiful.
A vista é maravilhosa!The view is wonderful!
É um lugar bem tranquilo.It's a really peaceful place.
Vale a pena visitar.It's worth visiting.
🇧🇷 Brazilians use "fazer + activity" a lot: fazer trilha, fazer mergulho, fazer rapel.
Think of "fazer" as "to do" — it pairs with almost any activity noun.
Dicas de Prática
Ways to practise with a speaker
1
Dream Trip
Describe your ideal holiday to your partner using the place vocabulary: "Eu quero ir para uma ilha com praia e cachoeira." They ask follow-up questions about activities.
2
Tour Guide Role-Play
One person plays the tour guide ("Aqui temos o centro histórico..."), the other plays the tourist asking questions ("Tem passeio guiado?" "Quanto custa?").
3
Photo Geography
Show travel photos or Google Maps images and name everything you see in Portuguese — a praia, o morro, a igreja, o rio. Race to identify the most places.
4
Activity Bucket List
Take turns saying "Eu quero fazer..." with a different activity each time. If you repeat one or can't think of a new one, you lose. Great for memorising activity vocab.
5
Trip Recap
Describe a real trip you took, but in Portuguese. Combine this lesson with the past tense lesson — "Fui para a serra, fiz trilha e vi uma cachoeira incrível!"
